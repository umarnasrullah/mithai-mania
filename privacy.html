Privacy Policy – Mithai Mania

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Mithai Mania ("the game") is made by M. Umar Nasrullah ("we"). This policy explains what information is used when you play.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress stays only on your device: - levels and stars - coins and boosters - shop upgrades - daily-reward streak - achievements - the nickname you type - your settings

Uninstalling the game or choosing Settings → Reset progress deletes it. We have no accounts and no servers that store your progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When ads are switched on in the game, they come from Google AdMob: - optional reward videos - occasional ads between levels, which the Remove Ads purchase turns off

To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about your interactions with ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent first using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

AdMob's use of data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

When the game offers purchases, they are processed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To protect against fraud, the game sends the purchase token and product name of each purchase to our verification server. That server asks Google whether the purchase is genuine.

The verification server does not store personal information. It sends the purchase token to Google, receives the answer, and keeps only temporary technical logs from the hosting provider (Vercel or Google Cloud). Your device keeps the order IDs of credited purchases so the same purchase is never credited twice.

Fonts

On first launch the game downloads its fonts from Google Fonts (https://policies.google.com/privacy). After that, all levels play offline.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If we add features that use data (for example cloud saves or analytics), we will update this policy and the Play Store disclosures before releasing them.
